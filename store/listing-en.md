# Store listing — English (en-US)

## Product name
Baddel - Keyboard Language Fixer

## Description
Typed a whole message, then noticed your keyboard was on the wrong language? Instead of deleting it and starting over, select the text and press Ctrl + Alt + Space. "hgsghl ugd;l" becomes "السلام عليكم" instantly, and your keyboard switches language so you can keep typing.

Baddel works in every app — Word, Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp, browsers and more — and in both directions: Arabic typed on an English layout, and English typed on the Arabic layout.

Why Baddel:
• One keystroke fixes text in any app, with a shortcut you choose.
• No selection needed: with nothing selected, Baddel fixes only the latest wrong words on the line.
• Press the shortcut again to undo instantly.
• Uses the keyboard layouts installed on your PC, including Arabic 101, 102 and AZERTY.
• Handles Caps Lock and the capital letter Word and Outlook add at the start of a sentence.
• Tells "لا" apart from "gh" in English words such as light and thought.
• Puts your clipboard back the way it was.
• A built-in converter for text you receive from others.
• Arabic and English interface, light and dark themes.

Privacy first: Baddel doesn't monitor your typing and only acts when you press the shortcut. Everything happens on your PC, and it never connects to the internet.

## Restricted capability justification (Submission options → runFullTrust)
Baddel is a packaged desktop (WPF/.NET) application; runFullTrust is required for all packaged desktop apps. The app registers one global keyboard shortcut. Only when the user presses it, Baddel copies the selected text, converts it on the device and pastes it back. It doesn't monitor keystrokes and makes no network connections.
